# TAVRN-BLE deviation and decision register

Status: complete normative companion to `TAVRN-BLE-V2.3`

Frozen: 2026-08-05

This register makes every known intentional mismatch explicit. “Accepted” means
normative for this proof of concept. “Deferred” and “out of scope” are deliberate
non-implementations, not unresolved questions.

`DEV-027` correction: complete validation precedes all TC cache mutation. A
same UUID is duplicate even if its event changes. A fresh UUID for a live same
`{subject,event}` is retained but suppresses local apply/relay; a fresh UUID with
the other event is eligible because JOIN and LEAVE have distinct subject keys.
The shared local TC ticket/commit seam replaces mentorship's private
`next_join_sequence` and is used by both the existing JOIN enqueue and LEAVE.
Delayed targeted tier-2 reservations consume one of the four shared slots but
are capacity-only and never general-RREQ/RREP/RERR metadata. General metadata is
attached to and parsed from real admitted SID8 route controls; synthetic policy
selection alone is insufficient. The three only TC LEAVE runtime sources are
terminal verification checked departure, direct-timeout checked departure, and
router/link `RETRY_EXHAUSTED` carrying failed-next-hop AdvA.

`DEV-027` corrective production-composition freeze: the TC origin UUID serial
is never a GTT subject freshness serial. JOIN/LEAVE preserve an existing subject
serial/presence, while TC-only admission has no subject serial. An admitted
general metadata answer resolves and refreshes its subject as imported
non-direct liveness, preserves subject serial/presence, applies the bounded
`min(gtt_hard_expiry, (bucket + 1) * 20 s)` lifetime, and clears the matching
soft request atomically. Invalid, stale, unknown, or colliding input is fully
non-mutating; metadata cannot grant direct provenance or resurrect a departed
tombstone without newer subject evidence. The shared four-slot pool gives
same-subject delayed contexts distinct exact-release ownership. Ordinary soft
expiry creates one retained request candidate, and confirmed ordinary direct
timeout emits one LEAVE even without application demand. FULL maintenance is the
single composed TC admission/apply/relay owner; mentorship keeps only the
bootstrap-JOIN compatibility seam. The `--green` runner exercises these
production-composed paths; `--red` preserves the inert legacy missing-policy
contract while requiring the production composition to remain green.

The corrective queue contract additionally retains distinct local LEAVEs and
distinct inbound controlled-flood relays in FIFO order across BUSY rather than
overwriting either pending fact. A retained LEAVE arbitrates behind an accepted
mentorship JOIN ticket and subsequently emits exactly once with the next
